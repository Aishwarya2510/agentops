# Billing Portal Login Support SOP

Owner: Customer Service Operations
Last Reviewed: 2025-12-20
Status: Active
Region: Global

## Problem
Customers cannot access the Billing Portal.

## Standard Troubleshooting
1. Confirm the customer email matches the billing contact on the account.
2. Check whether the account has Billing Portal enabled.
3. Ask the customer to clear cache and retry.
4. If SSO is enabled, route to IT Support.
5. If the customer is not the billing contact, request account admin confirmation.
6. If the issue persists, open a Billing Portal support case.

## Escalation
- Billing Portal access issue: Customer Service Ops
- Account contact mismatch: Account Admin / Sales Ops
- SSO issue: IT Support
- Invoice visibility issue: Billing Ops
- Payment failure issue: Payments Ops

## Common Error Messages
- "User not found"
- "Account not linked"
- "Permission denied"
- "Invoice unavailable"
- "Payment method failed"

## Known Gaps
Some legacy accounts migrated from older systems may not have correct billing contact mappings.
