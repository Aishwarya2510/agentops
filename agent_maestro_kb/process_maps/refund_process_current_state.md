# Current-State Refund Process Map

1. Customer contacts support requesting refund.
2. Customer Service searches knowledge articles.
3. If article is unclear, Customer Service asks Billing Ops in Slack.
4. Billing Ops checks invoice and payment.
5. Cash App checks payment receipt if needed.
6. Finance approver is contacted manually.
7. Approval is recorded in spreadsheet or case comment.
8. Customer is notified manually.
9. Refund is processed.
10. Case is closed.

## Pain Points
- Too many handoffs.
- Approval threshold confusion.
- Slack decisions not always documented.
- Regional exceptions unclear.
- No standard confidence or risk scoring.
- Customers may receive delayed or inconsistent communication.
