# Common Enterprise Operations Pain Points

These are realistic problems many organizations face.

## Process Issues
- Different teams follow different versions of the same process.
- Documentation is outdated or too long.
- Process maps do not reflect reality.
- Exceptions live in Slack, not official SOPs.
- New hires rely on tribal knowledge.

## System Issues
- CRM, finance system, billing portal, and knowledge base do not stay aligned.
- Data fields are inconsistent across tools.
- Dashboards show lagging indicators only.
- Approval evidence is spread across comments, spreadsheets, and emails.

## Governance Issues
- No clear owner for regional exceptions.
- Controls are manual.
- Risk is identified after problems occur.
- Teams create new manual workarounds without automation roadmap.

## AI Adoption Issues
- AI agents cannot answer because content is unstructured.
- AI outputs are not evaluated.
- Employees do not trust AI because sources are unclear.
- No feedback loop converts AI failures into process improvement.

## Business Impact
- Slower cycle time.
- Inconsistent customer experience.
- Higher audit risk.
- More escalations.
- Lower self-service adoption.
- Poor visibility for leadership.
