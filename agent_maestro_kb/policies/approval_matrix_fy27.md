# FY27 Approval Matrix - Finance Operations

Owner: Global Finance Governance
Last Reviewed: 2026-02-01
Status: Active

## Refund Approval
| Scenario | Approval Required | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Refund under $1,000 | Billing Ops Specialist | Billing Manager if disputed |
| Refund $1,000 - $4,999 | Regional Finance Manager | Director RevOps if customer escalated |
| Refund $5,000 - $24,999 | Director RevOps | VP Finance if strategic account |
| Refund $25,000+ | VP Finance + Legal | CFO review if material impact |
| Bankruptcy-related refund | Credit Risk + Legal | VP Finance |
| Fraud suspected | Risk Operations | Security + Legal |

## Payment Link / Quote Payment Approval
Payment links above $10,000 require manager validation.
Payment links tied to contract amendments require Revenue Operations review.

## Change Control
Any change to thresholds must be:
1. documented,
2. approved by Finance Governance,
3. communicated to Billing, Cash App, Customer Service, and Collections,
4. reflected in all related SOPs within 10 business days.

## Known Issue
Some downstream SOPs still show legacy refund thresholds. Use this matrix as source of truth.
