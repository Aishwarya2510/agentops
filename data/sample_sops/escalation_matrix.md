# Escalation Matrix SOP

Operational requests should be routed by risk and customer impact.

- Low risk: Team Lead owns resolution within two business days.
- Medium risk: Operations Manager owns resolution within one to two business days.
- High risk: Director-level owner required the same business day.

Escalate to Finance Controls when approval thresholds are exceeded, evidence is
incomplete, or policy interpretation is unclear. Escalate to Customer Success
Leadership when the issue affects a strategic account or customer trust.
