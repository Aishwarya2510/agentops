# Billing and Login Issue SOP

Billing issues should be validated against invoice history, payment processor
records, account credits, and recent subscription changes.

Login-related billing complaints require confirmation that account access,
identity verification, and billing ownership are aligned. Duplicate billing
claims should be reviewed within one business day and escalated if customer
access is blocked.

Recommended evidence includes invoice ID, payment timestamp, account email,
error screenshots, and customer support ticket history.
