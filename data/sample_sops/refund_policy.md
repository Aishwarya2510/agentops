# Refund Policy SOP

Refund requests must include customer identifier, transaction date, refund amount,
reason code, and evidence of service failure or duplicate charge.

Approval rules:
- Up to $500: Support Lead approval.
- $501 to $5,000: Operations Manager approval.
- Above $5,000: Finance Director approval and Finance Controls review.

High-risk refund requests require a documented decision note, customer impact
summary, and reconciliation check before funds are released.
