# Bad Debt Write-Off SOP

Owner: Credit Operations
Last Reviewed: 2025-09-10
Status: Active but under review

## Workflow
1. Identify account eligible for write-off.
2. Confirm collections attempts completed.
3. Verify dispute status.
4. Confirm bankruptcy/legal status.
5. Submit write-off request.
6. Obtain required approval.
7. Update finance system.
8. Archive supporting documentation.

## Required Approval
Write-offs under $5,000: Credit Manager.
Write-offs $5,000 - $49,999: Director Credit.
Write-offs $50,000+: VP Finance.

## Known Issues
- Some collectors skip documenting final outreach.
- Some cases lack evidence of dispute resolution.
- Approval tracking is inconsistent between spreadsheets and finance system.
- Reporting may not reflect write-off status in real time.
