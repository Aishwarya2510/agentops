# Journey to Marketing Cloud - Launch Readiness Runbook

Owner: Digital Transformation / Customer Communications
Last Reviewed: 2026-02-10
Status: Draft

## Purpose
Ensure operational readiness before launching customer communication journeys through Marketing Cloud.

## Go / No-Go Criteria
1. UAT complete.
2. Stakeholder sign-off received.
3. Help articles updated.
4. Customer communication templates approved.
5. Rollback plan documented.
6. Hypercare owners assigned.
7. Monitoring dashboard available.
8. Support teams trained.

## Risk Areas
- Incomplete customer segmentation.
- Broken links in communication templates.
- Incorrect billing contact data.
- Duplicate emails sent to customers.
- Lack of support readiness.
- No rollback owner assigned.

## Hypercare Plan
For the first 2 weeks after launch:
- daily monitoring,
- support ticket review,
- broken link tracking,
- customer complaint triage,
- weekly leadership update.

## Known Gap
The runbook does not yet define severity thresholds for launch rollback.
