# Cash Application - Unapplied Cash SOP

Owner: Cash Application Team
Last Reviewed: 2026-01-08
Status: Active
Region: Global

## Purpose
Guide Cash App team on handling unapplied cash.

## Workflow
1. Identify payment received without clear invoice match.
2. Search remittance details.
3. Match customer account.
4. If invoice is known, apply cash.
5. If invoice is unknown, route to Collections for customer outreach.
6. If payment is duplicate, route to Billing Ops for refund review.
7. If payment relates to dispute, route to Credit/Risk.
8. Track unresolved unapplied cash weekly.

## SLA
- Standard matching: 3 business days.
- Complex regional case: 7 business days.
- Strategic account: escalate within 1 business day.

## Common Issues
- missing remittance,
- wrong invoice number,
- payment applied to old account,
- duplicate payment,
- acquired customer account mismatch,
- currency mismatch.

## Automation Opportunity
AI can classify unapplied cash cases by root cause and suggest owner/action.
