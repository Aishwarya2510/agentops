# Collections Escalation SOP

Owner: Collections Operations
Last Reviewed: 2025-11-30
Status: Active

## Purpose
Define how overdue invoices and payment disputes are escalated.

## Workflow
1. Collector reviews overdue account.
2. Check invoice status, payment promises, and disputes.
3. If customer claims payment was made, route to Cash App.
4. If customer disputes invoice, route to Billing Ops.
5. If customer is strategic account, notify Account Executive.
6. If bankruptcy is suspected, route to Credit/Risk.
7. If over 60 days overdue, manager review required.

## Escalation Matrix
| Scenario | Owner |
|---|---|
| Payment made but not applied | Cash App |
| Invoice dispute | Billing Ops |
| Contract mismatch | Sales Ops |
| Bankruptcy | Credit/Risk + Legal |
| Strategic account escalation | AE + Collections Manager |

## Known Problem
Collectors often bypass standard routing and DM SMEs directly in Slack, causing poor auditability.
