# Global Refund Policy - RevOps Finance

Owner: Finance Operations
Last Reviewed: 2025-10-15
Status: Active but needs FY27 review
Region: Global
Audience: Internal

## Purpose
This policy defines how refund requests are reviewed, approved, documented, and communicated to customers.

## Standard Refund Approval Thresholds

| Refund Amount | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| $0 - $999 | Billing Operations Specialist |
| $1,000 - $4,999 | Regional Finance Manager |
| $5,000 - $24,999 | Director, Revenue Operations |
| $25,000+ | VP Finance + Legal Review |

## Required Documentation
All refund requests must include:
- customer account ID,
- invoice number,
- refund reason,
- region,
- payment method,
- evidence of overpayment or valid credit,
- approval record,
- customer communication.

## Standard Workflow
1. Customer submits refund request.
2. Billing Ops validates invoice and payment status.
3. Cash App confirms payment receipt and unapplied cash status.
4. Finance approver reviews refund amount.
5. Customer Service sends refund timeline communication.
6. Refund is processed.
7. Case is closed after confirmation.

## Important Controls
Refunds above $5,000 require documented approval before processing.
Refunds above $25,000 require Legal review.
Refunds related to bankruptcy or disputed invoices must be routed to Credit/Risk first.

## Known Risk
Some older regional SOPs still reference a $10,000 director approval threshold. That threshold is outdated.
