## Refund Thresholds
Refunds under $2,000 can be approved by Billing Ops.
Refunds $2,000 - $9,999 require Regional Manager approval.
Refunds $10,000+ require Director approval.

## Steps
1. Check customer account.
2. If refund is valid, process immediately if under $2,000.
3. For amounts above $10,000, email Director.
4. Notify customer after refund is complete.
